Privacy Policy

We built Tuck to keep link shortening simple. This page explains what information is handled when you use the Tuck demo.

Information stored

Links created in this browser are saved locally using browser storage. Destination URLs and custom endings are not sent to a Tuck server in the demo.

Browser storage

You can remove saved links at any time through the Tuck interface or by clearing this site's browser storage.

Third-party services

Tuck may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to that provider to retrieve and display the font files.
